Most budget apps ask for your bank login.
Owwy asks for nothing.

Every number lives in your phone's own storage. There is no sign-up, no cloud database, and no analytics watching what you spend. That is not a setting you switch on — it is how the app is built. The trade is simple and honest: you own the data, so you also own the backups.

Features

Everything a month of money needs

Built around the questions you actually ask — not a ledger with a chart bolted on.

  • The Grove

    Each spending category is a tree that grows through the month, so you read your month as a shape before a single number. It fills the screen, so on a phone it starts off and is one tap away in Customize home.

  • Safe to Spend

    Balances minus bills, debt minimums, savings plans, goal money and your cushion — until your next income. Every line is shown.

  • Scan a receipt

    Read on the device — shop, total, date and line items — then filed the way you usually file that shop. It shows the line the total came from.

  • Ask Owwy

    “Where did my money go last month?” answered from your own records — and Owwy shows the query it ran. A dozen starter questions to tap, every one of them answered on the device. On-device by default; bring a key from any AI service, or your own local model, if you want more.

  • Shopping trips

    Set a budget before you walk in, add things as they go in the trolley, and watch what's left. Owwy says which items would bring you back under.

  • Make it your colour

    Seven palettes repaint the buttons, the links, the active tab and the owl itself — from the forest green it ships with to navy, plum, or nearly no colour at all. The warm paper and the night forest behind your text stay exactly as they are, so nothing becomes harder to read.

  • Your wallet as a deck

    Every account is a card, stacked so the whole wallet is on one screen with each one’s top edge showing — tap a card behind and it deals itself forward. The big total above has ten finishes and five engravings, and wears the same one on every screen that shows a total. A grid and a plain list are still there if you prefer them.

  • Budgets you'll recognise

    A limit per category, with pace against the month and a warning before you go over. Give a catch-all like “Other” a name — pet supplies, the car — so it still means something in March. Logging one asks the same question: choose “Other” and Owwy asks what it actually was.

  • A keypad where the amount is

    The number pad opens with the sheet, so logging is tap-tap-save without the keyboard sliding over it. It does the arithmetic too — type 120+45×2 and use the answer. When a keyboard does open, it floats over the page rather than resizing it: nothing jumps, and nothing is left shifted when it closes.

  • Stats that explain

    Month-end forecast, category breakdown, net-worth trend, cash flow and a six-month projection — plus a heatmap of every day you logged.

  • Split a bill

    Your share is logged as spending; everyone else's becomes money owed to you — counted in net worth, not your budget, until it comes back.

  • Bills, plans, automations

    Salary, rent, “to savings every 1st” — logged without being asked. Pick several days a month when payday lands on the 15th and the 30th. Instalment plans show months paid, months left and a warning when you fall behind.

How it works

Four steps, then it mostly runs itself

  1. 01

    Open it — there's no sign-up

    No email, no password, no verification code. Start with sample data to look around, then clear it in one tap.

  2. 02

    Add accounts and what's due

    Wallets, bank accounts, e-wallets, cash and credit. Add the bills you know about so Owwy can hold money back for them.

  3. 03

    Log it however is fastest

    Type coffee at a shop 180 gcash and the whole entry fills itself. Scan a receipt. Tap a repeat. Or let an automation do it.

  4. 04

    Check one number before you spend

    Safe to Spend already accounts for everything coming. Thinking about a purchase? Ask “can I afford it?” and see before and after.

Why it's built this way

The benefits of keeping it on your device

  • Your finances stay yours

    There is no account to breach and no server to leak, because neither exists. The only copy is on your phone and the backups you choose to make.

  • It works with no signal

    Underground, on a plane, or on a bad connection — the whole app is on the device, including the receipt reader.

  • Nothing to unsubscribe from

    No ads, no upsell interrupting a balance check, no engagement notifications designed to pull you back in.

  • Built for pesos first

    ₱ is the default and the maths is built around it, with multi-currency accounts when you need them.

  • You can always leave

    Export to CSV or JSON whenever you like. Encrypted backups travel with you. Your data is not a hostage.

  • Honest about what it is

    Owwy does budgeting maths. It is not financial advice, it does not sell you products, and it never pretends a guess is a fact.

FAQ

Questions worth asking

Is it really free?

The app is free to use and there are no ads. There is no account, so there is nothing to bill and no plan to cancel.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's own storage on your device, using IndexedDB. It is never uploaded. If you clear your browser data for the site, it is gone — which is why Owwy keeps seven daily on-device snapshots and makes exporting a backup one tap.

Do you connect to my bank?

No, and it is not planned. Owwy never asks for banking credentials and has no integration that could request them. You record what you spend, or scan the receipt.

How does receipt scanning work without a server?

The text-recognition engine is downloaded once (about 7 MB) and then runs inside your browser. The photo is never uploaded. Owwy always shows what it read — including which line it took the total from — and nothing is saved until you confirm.

Is this financial advice?

No. Owwy does arithmetic on numbers you give it. “Can I afford it?” compares a purchase against your own balances and commitments; it is a calculation, not a recommendation, and not a substitute for a licensed adviser.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Not automatically — there is no sync, because there is no server. You can move your data by exporting a backup and importing it on the other device.

What about the AI features?

Ask Owwy answers on your device by default, with no network access at all. If you want a more conversational answer you can add your own API key from whichever AI service you prefer — or point it at a model running on your own machine, in which case nothing leaves the device either. Only then does anything get sent, it goes straight from your device to the service you chose, and you decide whether notes are included.

Will there be an Android or iPhone app?

That's the plan. The app is already built to be packaged for the Play Store and the App Store, and the web version can be installed to your home screen today — it runs offline and behaves like an installed app.
